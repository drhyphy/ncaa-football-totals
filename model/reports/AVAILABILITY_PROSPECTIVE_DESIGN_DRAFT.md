# ACC availability information: prospective design draft

**DRAFT / UNFROZEN — revised September 9, 2026. NOT ACTIVATED.** This proposes one directional probability experiment using a separately frozen collection pilot. It does not authorize a fitted candidate, revise an existing betting policy, or establish an edge. No target-game movement/outcome labels or real-data fits informed this revision. The original draft is preserved in Git commit `65f7fb377b1b938338eaf6b19aa8ebea960e698e`; this revision corrects its game-day-only transition design, which could miss a quarterback already listed Out in the initial report. The source-only follow-up supports prior passing-attempt shares as the intended role representation. The populated ACC player schema, crosswalk and final model protocol remain unresolved and must be frozen before target labels are examined or fitting begins.

## Existing collection and proposed study inputs

The [fixed collection protocol](ACC_AVAILABILITY_CAPTURE_PROTOCOL.md) admits starts in **[September 9, 2026 08:00 UTC, September 16, 2026 07:00 UTC)** and contains **245 nominal slots**: `:17`/`:47` at hours 00–02 and 10–23, plus 06:17, in America/New_York. It stores separate `data/runtime/availability` archives and labels manual observations separately. This draft does not change that grid or window. Scheduler delays, daytime/night gaps, source caching and late publication limit detection. A season continuation would need its own protocol before collection/evaluation under it; the seven-day pilot does not guarantee enough distinct games for a model fit.

The implemented collector freezes a seven-day official ESPN inventory before requesting availability. It matches the **unordered** source team pair against exact normalized official name variants and requires literal source footer date/time agreement with kickoff under the explicit operational interpretation `ET = America/New_York`. This is not certification of the feed's clock semantics or the venue-local policy deadline. It does not infer home/away from block order, equate report keys with ESPN IDs or silently repair discrepant dates/times. An official summary recheck precedes prices. Ambiguous reports remain archived but unresolved; roof and neutral-site exclusions do not apply. Optional nonconference reports are retained. The proposed model's primary cohort is verified ACC conference games; that classification still needs an explicit source contract.

The collector requests prices for reconciled reports, including pending ones, rather than the national 24-hour board. It retains exact same-book full-game paired Over/Under lines and decimal prices from the already selected DraftKings/FanDuel books. One paired book suffices for a research observation; two-book coverage is diagnostic and permits an optional same-contract offer comparison. The 20-game cap, ordered by kickoff/game ID before odds, limits IO calls to four and all HTTP calls to 27. The [versioned quota amendment](COLLECTION_QUOTA_METADATA_AMENDMENT.md) permits those fixed calls when allowance headers are absent, while preserving reported-low-allowance and 401/403/429 stops. Missing headers do not establish remaining quota or guarantee a 20-request reserve. Earlier refused captures remain unchanged; later prices cannot fill them retroactively.

Each receipt preserves UTC request/response times, HTTP method and public JSON body, response headers including `Date`, `Age`, `Cache-Control`, `ETag` and `Last-Modified`, original decoded response bytes, hashes, protocol/code version and run/attempt identity. Current source caching permits 60-second caching and longer stale service; a new HTTP receipt is not certified publication or a server-fresh status change. Store source issue labels separately. Fetch quotes **after** the report receipt and successful current official matchup reconciliation, preserving report-to-quote latency. Do not back-pair an earlier quote as though it followed the report. The source's embedded imagery may dominate bytes: deduplicate raw bodies by hash while retaining every receipt.

## First-received revisions and a later source-mechanism check

After a populated current body is audited, freeze a semantic representation containing only verified report phase, canonical game/team identity and player/status fields. Keep full raw hashes independently. A logo, font or field-order change is not player news. Track first receipt of each semantic version within each game/phase, its immediately preceding observed version and any missing intervals. A return to an older version is a recorded reversion, not a new favorable first release. Pending-to-completed, first-observed-without-prior, completed-to-revised, unchanged and source disappearance are separate states. Never replace the earliest observed changed version because a later receipt has better prices. Different players sharing a name/jersey or unresolved roster matches remain ambiguous.

The collection pilot reports coverage and timing only; it does not inspect movement labels. A separately registered **secondary, no-fit information-content check** could ask whether first completed/revised reports are followed by further same-book main-line movement at the immediately next scheduled half-hour observation. Define signed movement `L_next − L_now` and magnitude `abs(L_next − L_now)`; retain integer totals. Require actual quote separation 15–45 minutes and future kickoff. Select the next archived scheduled slot before validating its quotes; a failed/missing next observation has no later replacement. Choose the reference book once at the starting observation, DraftKings then FanDuel, and keep that book for the target. Use only a unique main total; multiple-line ambiguity remains explicit rather than selecting by future movement.

Report movements for changed, unchanged and pending observations, with game-equal summaries and all missing denominators; show signed changes and same-line paired-price changes separately. Their temporal/control groups are observational, not randomized: scheduled news, selection of injured players, simultaneous information and sportsbook anticipation can explain associations. Many snapshots of one game are not independent games. No threshold or horizon search follows from this pilot. Movement magnitude can test whether this sampling regime captures remaining price adjustment; **it supplies neither direction nor a win probability and cannot generate EV picks.**

## Smallest directional extension to finalize after the schema audit

Propose **one** market-offset logistic reference and one availability challenger, used at **two fixed opportunities per game**: the first archived scheduled receipt of a verified completed initial report, and the first such receipt of its verified completed game-day report. Designate each opportunity from phase/completion metadata before examining player statuses, prices, role coverage or predictions. A failed designated opportunity is retained as unavailable, never replaced by a later favorable version or offer. Manual receipts remain provenance/history rather than primary forecast opportunities. The exact completion/phase predicate must be verified and frozen first; a Pending or merely nonpending body cannot designate a completed report.

At each opportunity choose the reference book by availability, DraftKings then FanDuel, with a unique paired **half-point** total. The target is the official final total finishing Under that exact line, including overtime. Define `q=(1/d_under)/(1/d_under+1/d_over)` as a market reference, not known truth. Shared controls are current total, `log1p(hours_to_kickoff)`, the same-book preceding half-hour total change, verified report phase, and a missing-prior-report indicator. The reference and challenger use identical covered observations and controls.

The availability block contains exactly two predictors: **current full-game-Out QB burden `B_t`**, and **its change `B_t − B_previous`** from the immediately preceding verified completed same-game report actually received before the current one. Compute both burdens using the same pre-decision role definition and weights, so a changing player universe cannot masquerade as a status revision. For an initial report without a prior completed report, retain the known current burden, set the change placeholder to zero and set the shared missing-history indicator. This does not assert that the earlier burden was zero. Missing current status/identity evidence remains unknown, not an invented zero burden. Partial-game suspensions stay separate and receive no full-game-Out weight. Uncertain categories are not assigned policy percentages as probabilities.

**The intended role representation is now prior passing-attempt share, chosen from source feasibility before target labels or fitting.** For each verified roster QB with at least one passing attempt in his team's most recent completed current-season game, use his attempts divided by that game's published team attempts. The denominator includes every passing athlete, including non-QBs, and must reconcile with the full player table. Sum the weights of full-game-Out QBs across the two teams. The same fixed pre-decision weights define both current and previous burden. These shares measure previous participation, not expected snaps, starter certainty or player quality. Do not test both shares and unweighted counts and retain the better result. The populated ACC schema, exact identity crosswalk and listed-versus-policy-presumed availability treatment still require verification; name/jersey similarity alone is insufficient. A missing prior game, nonpositive/unreconciled team denominator or unresolved player identity is unavailable evidence, not an invented zero. Only role/participation receipts actually available before the decision may enter; target-game play or post-decision roster information cannot determine the QB set. If these joins cannot be established, continue collection without fabricating directional inputs. The two-team source check does not certify coverage for every future team.

For the proposed pair, `logit(p)=logit(q)+alpha+beta'C`, with the challenger adding `gamma'A` for current burden and change. Give each distinct training game total weight one across its eligible opportunities; minimize the resulting game-weighted mean log loss plus `0.1/2` times all squared fitted coefficients, including the intercept. Use the same game weights for training-only predictor means/population SDs, constant scale one and clipping at ±5. Coefficients have unrestricted signs and strong shrinkage toward zero: no QB absence is assigned a known negative scoring effect. No category probability or penalty grid is proposed. Zero-variation columns remain constant/zero; rare changes are not another eligibility gate. The estimate may be weak or null.

An achievable **proposed operational minimum is 20 distinct covered games across two completed Eastern weeks**, with both Under/non-Under labels represented. Multiple opportunities do not count as extra games. This permits experimental estimation, not a calibration or profitability claim; positive L2 identifies a numerical fit but cannot create information about rare QB absences. One-book and unchanged-report observations remain eligible, and a valid initial opportunity does not require a later game-day opportunity to succeed. The initial burden can therefore inform the model even when the first report already lists a QB Out and no later transition occurs. Report the actual role/status variation and unavailable opportunities without adding an outcome-selected sample gate.

Refit on fixed Monday 00:00 Eastern cutoffs using only previously completed kickoff weeks and final-score receipts actually available before the cutoff. Keep all observations of each game in the same chronological fold; no game's earlier opportunity may train its later one. Both models use identical games and opportunities. Predictions require artifacts available before their new quotes; preserve transforms, source hashes and original predictions. Evaluate every eligible future forecast pair by log loss and Brier, including the raw `q` reference, averaging opportunities within game before aggregating across games. Report each phase and its missingness as well. Paired uncertainty must resample whole kickoff weeks with all game observations together, not treat two opportunities as independent. The proposed January 31, 2027 endpoint and February 8 report require a frozen continuation/forecast protocol before use. No interim promotion, ROI selection or pooling with existing policy families is proposed.

The eventual economic test permits **at most one flat-unit paper entry per game: the earliest designated opportunity with a qualifying positive-EV offer**. At its modeled half-point line, Under estimated EV is `d*p−1`, Over is `d*(1−p)−1`; select the maximum positive estimated EV, fixed ties Under then Over and DraftKings then FanDuel. A peer book at a different line cannot use this probability. Revalidate the same invocation's original chosen offer within 120 seconds before immutable lock; observed prices do not establish acceptance. Once an initial entry is locked, a later opportunity cannot replace, improve or add to it, although its forecast remains in the proper-score evaluation. Retain all losses and unresolved outcomes with their original unit risk. Better proper scores, movement or CLV alone do not meet the profitability goal. No paper policy is activated by this draft.

The roughly half-hour cadence tests mispricing remaining when this client receives a report; it may miss all immediate market adjustment, even at the initial opportunity. It cannot identify first public dissemination, other information arriving simultaneously, accepted prices or a causal injury effect. Current Pending captures establish none of the still-missing populated schema, reliable role joins, informative status variation, probability improvement or profitable returns. Faster polling would require a separate timing protocol, never retrospective backdating of these receipts.
