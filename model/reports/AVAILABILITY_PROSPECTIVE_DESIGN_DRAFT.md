# ACC availability information: prospective design draft

**DRAFT / UNFROZEN — September 9, 2026.** This recommends a bounded collection pilot and one later directional probability experiment. It does not authorize a fitted candidate, revise an existing policy, or establish an edge. No new report/player values, outcome joins or real-data fits were used to choose this design. Collection can begin while the populated player-row schema is being verified; the model specification must be completed and frozen before examining its labels or fitting.

## What is established, and what can begin now

The [current preflight](ACC_CURRENT_AVAILABILITY_PREFLIGHT.md) verified the public POST route, an explicitly **Report Pending** response, two named team blocks and their empty `rows`. It did not verify a populated current player-row schema, stable game/player IDs, complete-report marker or revision numbering. The opaque key `1182` is not a canonical event ID. The [archive](ACC_AVAILABILITY_PREFLIGHT.md) provides status categories and name/jersey fields, but no trustworthy historical publication timeline. Do not reconstruct earlier actionable reports from its final phase columns.

The [verified 2026 policy](ACC_AVAILABILITY_POLICY_2026.md) requires reports three, two and one nights before the game by **20:00 in the game venue's local time**, then no later than two hours before kickoff. Both teams are published together after the deadline; the policy gives no maximum publication lag. First-half suspension is distinct from full-game Out. Its category percentages are policy descriptions, not measured participation probabilities. The policy's unlisted-player presumption applies to a completed applicable report; Pending, missing, invalid or unverified-complete reports cannot imply availability.

Archive the current public response followed by contemporaneous quotes now. Classify source states separately as pending, verified completed, unknown schema/completion, HTTP failure, absent expected report and ambiguous matchup. None needs a player-role join to retain its original bytes and price observations. A later parser can reconstruct research inputs from those original receipts; it cannot create a historical forecast or paper entry.

## Bounded pilot proposal

Use a separate `acc-availability-pilot-v1` archive, with actual start through **September 16, 2026 at 03:00 America/New_York**; freeze the exact start before the first pilot request. Proposed daily slots are every half hour at `:17`/`:47`, from **18:17 through 02:17 Eastern**, plus 06:17. Add the same half-hour grid on game day from kickoff minus 150 minutes through kickoff minus 30 minutes for covered games. Deduplicate overlapping slots. This includes pre-deadline controls, Eastern evening reports, Pacific evening reports and their later receipts. It does not promise immediate discovery or coverage of every late publication. Manual observations remain separately identified.

Freeze the run's official ESPN inventory for the next seven days before parsing availability or prices. Match each report's **unordered** pair of reviewed canonical team names to a unique official event, using its source footer date and the official venue-local game date. Do not infer home/away from team-block order, equate the report key with ESPN ID, or reinterpret literal `ET` metadata as a UTC timestamp. Preserve source/official date, time and location discrepancies; ambiguity leaves the raw report retained and the join unresolved. Roof and neutral-site exclusions from the weather study have no role here. Retain optional nonconference reports in a separate cohort; the proposed primary study is ACC-versus-ACC conference games.

Request prices for those reconciled reports, including pending ones, rather than the national 24-hour board. Preserve every same-book full-game paired Over/Under line and its exact decimal prices. Use the already selected DraftKings and FanDuel books; one valid paired book is sufficient for a primary research observation. Two-book coverage is a diagnostic and an optional same-contract offer comparison, not a universal eligibility gate. Cap matched events at 20 in kickoff/game-ID order, hence at most four IO requests per ordinary capture: selected books, events, and two ten-event batches. Honor actual rate-limit headers and the existing 20-request reserve across workflows; record quota failures without replacing missing prices later. Audit actual slate/call coverage before deciding on a separately registered season continuation.

Each receipt preserves UTC request/response times, HTTP method and public JSON body, response headers including `Date`, `Age`, `Cache-Control`, `ETag` and `Last-Modified`, original decoded response bytes, hashes, protocol/code version and run/attempt identity. Current source caching permits 60-second caching and longer stale service; a new HTTP receipt is not certified publication or a server-fresh status change. Store source issue labels separately. Fetch quotes **after** the report receipt and successful current official matchup reconciliation, preserving report-to-quote latency. Do not back-pair an earlier quote as though it followed the report. The source's embedded imagery may dominate bytes: deduplicate raw bodies by hash while retaining every receipt.

## First-received revisions and the pilot's falsifiable question

After a populated current body is audited, freeze a semantic representation containing only verified report phase, canonical game/team identity and player/status fields. Keep full raw hashes independently. A logo, font or field-order change is not player news. Track first receipt of each semantic version within each game/phase, its immediately preceding observed version and any missing intervals. A return to an older version is a recorded reversion, not a new favorable first release. Pending-to-completed, first-observed-without-prior, completed-to-revised, unchanged and source disappearance are separate states. Never replace the earliest observed changed version because a later receipt has better prices. Different players sharing a name/jersey or unresolved roster matches remain ambiguous.

The pilot's primary result is receipt coverage and usable transitions, not performance. A useful **secondary, no-fit information-content check** is whether first completed/revised reports are followed by further same-book main-line movement at the immediately next scheduled half-hour observation. Define signed movement `L_next − L_now` and magnitude `abs(L_next − L_now)`; retain integer totals. Require actual quote separation 15–45 minutes and future kickoff. Select the next archived scheduled slot before validating its quotes; a failed/missing next observation has no later replacement. Choose the reference book once at the starting observation, DraftKings then FanDuel, and keep that book for the target. Use only a unique main total; multiple-line ambiguity remains explicit rather than selecting by future movement.

Report movements for changed, unchanged and pending observations, with game-equal summaries and all missing denominators; show signed changes and same-line paired-price changes separately. Their temporal/control groups are observational, not randomized: scheduled news, selection of injured players, simultaneous information and sportsbook anticipation can explain associations. Many snapshots of one game are not independent games. No threshold or horizon search follows from this pilot. Movement magnitude can test whether this sampling regime captures remaining price adjustment; **it supplies neither direction nor a win probability and cannot generate EV picks.**

## Smallest directional extension to finalize after the schema audit

Propose one market-offset logistic reference and one availability challenger at an exact offered **half-point** total. The target is the official final total finishing Under, including overtime. For paired decimal prices define `q=(1/d_under)/(1/d_under+1/d_over)`; this is a market reference, not known truth. Shared controls are current total, `log1p(hours_to_kickoff)`, the same-book preceding half-hour total change, and the verified report phase. The challenger adds only two game-level indicators: a relevant quarterback explicitly changes from a known non-Out category to full-game Out; and a relevant quarterback explicitly changes from full-game Out to Available. Other uncertain-status transitions and first-half suspensions remain recorded but receive no invented numerical weights.

The proposed relevant-QB set is an exact official-roster QB match with at least one passing attempt in that team's most recent completed game, with both pieces of evidence actually received before the availability decision. It is a prior participation definition, not a claim about the next starter. This source/identity mapping is **not yet verified** and cannot be frozen from name/jersey guesses. Inspect a populated body and a bounded official roster/prior-participation source before fixing the row crosswalk, completion/phase mapping and treatment of listed versus policy-presumed availability. Preserve unknowns. Do not retrospectively select a quarterback because he played or scored in the target game. If this role source is unavailable, retain collection and the movement check; do not substitute an outcome-selected proxy or assume all Out counts lower scoring.

For the proposed pair, `logit(p)=logit(q)+alpha+beta'C`, with the challenger adding `gamma'A`. Use mean log loss plus `0.1/2` times all squared fitted coefficients, including the intercept; training-only mean/population-SD transforms, constant scale one and clipping at ±5. No sign constraint, category probability or penalty grid. Zero-variation status columns remain constant/zero; rare transitions are not an additional fit gate. This can produce a weak or null estimate, which must be reported.

An achievable **proposed operational minimum is 20 distinct covered games across two completed Eastern weeks**, with both final outcomes represented. This is a modest experimental start for a strongly penalized model, not sufficient evidence of calibration or profit; a larger arbitrary minimum would consume much of the finite ACC slate. Positive L2 identifies a numerical fit even with collinear columns, but cannot manufacture information about rare QB changes. One-book coverage and unchanged-report games remain eligible. Finalize one decision per game before viewing labels: the first scheduled observation of its applicable game-day report, selected before checking player changes, prices or model output; no later successful substitution. Earlier nightly versions provide the revision history. The exact designation/completion predicate must be frozen after schema verification.

Refit on fixed Monday 00:00 Eastern cutoffs using only previously completed weeks and final-score receipts actually available before the cutoff. Both models use identical games; predictions require an artifact already available before their new quote. Keep training transforms, source/version hashes and original predictions immutable. Evaluate all future forecast pairs by log loss and Brier, against each other and raw `q`; do not select on ROI. A proposed season endpoint is January 31, 2027, with one February 8 report; a continuation/forecast protocol must freeze dates and uncertainty rules before it begins. Weekly dependence and the small conference cohort limit confidence; do not pool this study with the four existing policies or the weather-revision family.

The eventual economic test must preserve every selected flat-unit paper position and actual offered price. At the modeled half-point line, Under estimated EV is `d*p−1`, Over is `d*(1−p)−1`; choose at most one strictly positive-EV offer, fixed ties Under then Over and DraftKings then FanDuel. A peer book at a different line cannot use this probability. Revalidate the same invocation's original chosen offer within 120 seconds before immutable lock; do not claim sportsbook acceptance. All losses and unresolved outcomes stay in the denominator. Better proper scores, directional movement or CLV alone do not satisfy the profitability goal. No paper policy is activated by this draft.
