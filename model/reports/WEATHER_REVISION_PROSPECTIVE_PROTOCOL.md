# Prospective weather-revision runner protocol

Protocol ID: `weather-revision-prospective-v1-20260909`. Recorded September 9, 2026 before this runner inspects new revision features, target labels or fitted real-data results. This protocol completes the operational details of the [fixed two-model design](WEATHER_REVISION_MODEL_DESIGN.md); it does not change its features, objectives or penalties. Existing operational coverage and source feasibility are known. No profitability result is claimed. The corresponding [machine contract](weather_revision_prospective_protocol.json) records the constants and source hashes; the publishing Git commit freezes this protocol and the reviewed runner implementation before first use.

The revision study has its own namespace, decision records, artifacts and paper ledger. Its probability policy is `weather-revision-probability-v1-20260909`; its movement experiment is `weather-revision-movement-v1-20260909`. Do not pool either with the [four existing policies](PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md), alter those policies, or reuse their frozen-family confidence claim.

## Observations and permanent game designation

Enumerate original archived scheduled captures in actual `capture_started_at` order, breaking exact ties by run ID then run attempt. Manual captures cannot designate games, supply the preceding scheduled observation or provide the next scheduled movement target. The primary observation for each canonical game is the **first archived scheduled capture starting 24–48 hours before its pre-weather-cohort kickoff**, inclusive. Designate it before inspecting weather, prices, labels, artifact availability or model output. Record every designation and every exclusion. Missing required input excludes that game from forecasting; a later successful capture cannot replace it. An unreadable archive that prevents ordering makes the affected designation unavailable until the original archive is repaired; repair permits training reconstruction, never retrospective paper entry. Unarchived scheduler invocations are unknown and are not reconstructed as nominal slots.

The older observation is the immediately preceding archived scheduled capture, without substituting an older successful one. Require actual capture separation of 4–8 hours, requested GFS initialization separation of exactly six hours, and the same canonical game/team/venue/roof/neutral/kickoff context, frozen coordinates, source, request options and four game-valid hours. Require outdoor/non-neutral venue evidence. Same-initialization changed bytes are not a weather revision. Keep the pilot and season collection profiles and their respective protocol hashes intact. Automated source, receipt, context and field validation determines eligibility; a separately authored human-readable audit for every capture is not an additional prerequisite.

At `t0`, require one unique DraftKings main full-game paired Over/Under line. At `t1`, require one such DraftKings pair and one FanDuel pair, with actual HTTP receipts after the current weather and successful official context recheck. Use original observed full-state prices; provider market-update timestamps do not replace HTTP receipts. The information cutoff `decision_observed_at` is the maximum of the two current book receipts. Both receipts must precede kickoff and that maximum must still be 24–48 hours before kickoff. No forward-filled tick, later price or different-line probability may fill a missing input. Retain source/body/receipt hashes and the full original context.

The shared controls, in order, are current DraftKings total; hours from `decision_observed_at` to kickoff; current four-hour mean scalar wind in mph; kickoff-hour temperature in Fahrenheit; kickoff-hour relative humidity in percent; DraftKings total change from `t0`; change in its proportional no-vig Under logit; and current FanDuel-minus-DraftKings total. The three revision features, in order, are current-minus-previous wind, temperature and RH using those same definitions. For paired prices, `q = (1/d_under) / (1/d_under + 1/d_over)`. This is a market reference, not known truth. Do not derive additional features, impose weather cutoffs or search alternative horizons.

## Fixed fits and artifact availability

Maintain independent probability and movement target families. Each has one shared-control reference and one reference-plus-revision challenger. Both versions within a target use identical training games; missing movement coverage cannot block a valid probability fit, and vice versa. Probability training requires a positive half-point DraftKings line; movement training may also use integer lines.

The probability model has fixed offset `logit(q)` with coefficient one. Minimize mean binary log loss plus `0.1/2` times the squared fitted coefficients, including intercept. The movement model minimizes mean squared error of the training-standardized target plus `0.1/2` times squared slopes, with an unpenalized intercept; a constant training target predicts its training mean. For each target/version, fit predictor means and population standard deviations on training only, use scale one for a constant predictor, and clip transformed values to `[-5,5]`. No imputation, hyperparameter search or coefficient adjustment. The existing deterministic utility and optimizer/failure checks are fixed by source hash.

Refit once per Eastern week with a **Monday 00:00 America/New_York data cutoff**. A delayed invocation may perform that week's fit using exactly this cutoff; it cannot create a forecast before the fit actually finishes. Include all previously designated eligible games from completed earlier Eastern Monday–Sunday kickoff weeks whose required target receipt is strictly before the cutoff. Within each game's target history, use the latest valid correction received strictly before that cutoff. A game and its repeated captures never cross its own training/prediction split. Require **60 distinct eligible games across at least two completed weeks**, independently for each target. This is an experimental fit minimum, not a confidence threshold. No first-fit date is promised.

Write the training IDs, feature/target receipt identities, selected correction versions, cutoff, transform, parameters, convergence status, software/source hashes and protocol hash into an immutable weekly artifact. `artifact_available_at` is the actual UTC time after its successful atomic write. Both the reference and revision artifacts for a target must already be available at or before **each** current input-book receipt. An artifact fitted after those quotes cannot produce a prospective forecast for that designation. A failed scheduled fit leaves that target unavailable for the new week; no old artifact is silently substituted. Operational retries may repeat the same frozen cutoff and inputs, retaining failed attempts, but cannot change parameters or use newly received labels in that week's fit.

As weeks pass, an earlier evaluated game's now-available target may enter a later fixed weekly fit. This is sequential evaluation of a fixed learning algorithm. Keep its original prediction unchanged; never recompute its historical score with newer coefficients, revised weather or a different offer. Older original observations may be reconstructed for training when their source and timing are validated. Such reconstruction never creates historical forecasts or paper bets, and a label collected now is available now, not at its historical game's end.

## Prospective predictions and the final offer check

Publish a prospective prediction only as a continuation of the **same live scheduled invocation** that produced its designated `t1` capture, with matching run ID and attempt. Verify the original inputs and artifact hashes, then record `inference_completed_at`. Both current book receipts must be at or before this time and no more than **120 seconds old**. If the runner starts too late, loses the invocation context, or finds an unavailable artifact, record the reason and abstain; later invocation or archive replay cannot make a prospective entry for that game. Training and coverage records remain available.

The probability forecast stores the reference and challenger logits/probabilities, `q`, exact reference line, features, original quote IDs, artifact IDs and their actual availability times. Compute `P(Under)=p`, `P(Over)=1-p`, `P(push)=0` at that same half-point line. Store the movement reference/challenger forecasts independently when available. Its forecast cannot select a bet or change the probability policy.

For Candidate 1, enumerate only the original current DraftKings and FanDuel paired offers at the **exact DraftKings reference half-point line**. The two books may have different prices. At decimal odds `d`, Under EV is `d*p-1`, Over EV is `d*(1-p)-1`. Select at most one offer with strictly positive estimated EV, sorting by descending unrounded EV, then **Under before Over**, then **DraftKings before FanDuel**. This is a fixed zero-EV selection rule; no minimum-edge grid, weather threshold, top-N limit, bankroll scaling or Kelly formula is introduced. A finite probability and finite decimal price greater than one are mandatory. Different-line offers are unavailable to this model.

Immediately before atomically locking the selected paper position, revalidate its current-capture receipt/body hash, unique paired market, canonical game, pregame source state, exact side/line/decimal price and successful context linkage. The chosen receipt must still be at or before `paper_locked_at` and no more than **120 seconds old**, inference must have finished, and kickoff must remain future. This is a final freshness and integrity check of the newly fetched batch in the same invocation. **It does not require a second API request and does not claim that a sportsbook accepted or continuously held the offer.** There is no after-inference historical lookup, favorable price substitution or stale-offer renewal for this designation. A failed check yields no position. Record `offer_check_kind=current_capture_receipt`, its actual check time and quote age.

Risk one hypothetical unit per locked position. The immutable entry contains study/policy/protocol version, canonical game and context IDs, first-designation run/attempt, kickoff, receipt hashes, actual decision/inference/lock times, artifact IDs and availability times, side, line, decimal price, predicted win probability, estimated EV and one-unit risk. Existing entries are idempotent by study, policy and game ID. A duplicate attempt may confirm the existing entry but cannot replace its probability, line, price or time. A crash before durable lock creates no position; recovery must not invent an earlier lock. All valid forecasts, abstentions, unavailable states and selected losses remain in the record.

## Targets, corrections and paper grading

For final-total labels, archive the actual public official ESPN response and request/receipt metadata. Require exact event/competition IDs and home/away team identity, one competition, `status.type.completed=true` with `state=post`, and finite nonnegative integer final scores. Define total `Y=home_score+away_score`, including overtime as represented by the full-game final. A live score, assumed kickoff-plus-duration or retroactively downloaded final does not establish earlier availability. The training target's `target_available_at` is the actual successful receipt time. For a half-point reference, the binary label is `1[Y < L_1]`; a half-point contract cannot push against an integer total.

Store subsequent score corrections as append-only target versions with their own actual receipts. A correction arriving after a weekly cutoff can affect later fits only. It never rewrites a historical training manifest, forecast or price. At the fixed final report use the latest valid official target received at or before the report cutoff, identify corrections and show their effect on previously reported descriptive grading. Paper outcome/status updates are append-only references to the original position.

For movement, use DraftKings' unique main full-game line in the **immediately next archived scheduled capture** after `t1`. The target receipt must be 4–8 hours after `decision_observed_at` and before kickoff, with unchanged official context and a valid newly observed paired quote. A new weather measurement, FanDuel quote or usable model artifact at `t2` is not required for this price-only target. The next scheduled capture is selected before checking its validity; missing or failed input makes the target missing. Do not use a later capture, a manual run, the closing line or a retrospectively reported tick. Set `M=L_2-L_1`, with availability at that original `t2` quote receipt. A missing entire scheduler invocation remains unknowable; this definition uses archived scheduled captures consistently.

Grade paper wins as `decimal_odds-1` and losses as `-1` at the locked half-point contract. This study's simulated settlement follows the final total of the same official game; it is not a claim about a book's acceptance, postponement, cancellation or correction rules. Preserve any post-lock venue/kickoff change as a flag rather than deleting a losing prediction. Canceled games without a usable final, conflicting identities and unresolved records remain explicitly ungraded; do not invent a void or silently remove their unit risk. Report any established zero-return administrative resolution separately with its evidence.

## Fixed endpoint, comparisons and uncertainty

Include eligible future game kickoffs after the first usable artifact through **January 31, 2027 at 23:59:59 America/New_York**. The collection continuation remains separately bounded at February 1, 2027 at 03:00 UTC; postgame label collection is separate. Perform the single final study report at **February 8, 2027 at 12:00 UTC**, using targets received by that time. Preserve unresolved records. Running coverage, scores and returns may be descriptive, with no interim promotion, optional stopping, outcome-selected refit dates or extending this endpoint until significance appears.

The primary economic estimand is **aggregate profit divided by units risked across every selected, locked Candidate 1 paper position**. Each locked position contributes one unit to this cohort, including a subsequently unresolved or zero-return administrative record. If any position remains ungraded, the full-cohort ROI is incomplete: show settled-only ROI explicitly as a partial descriptive statistic, and show a conservative full-cohort return charging each unresolved position a full-unit loss. Do not silently assign unresolved profit zero. With no locked positions, ROI is unavailable rather than zero.

For the complete or unresolved-as-loss paper cohort, report 95% and 99% two-sided percentile whole-week bootstrap intervals and all active-week counts. These are approximate descriptive intervals, not a profitability certificate or a multiplicity-corrected promotion test. Profitability remains the goal even if forecast scores improve. Show book/side counts, team/venue concentration, receipt-age distributions, coverage losses and leave-one-week-out ROI. A positive estimate dominated by one week is fragile. No real-money staking or automatic policy promotion follows from this study.

The two fixed incremental model comparisons are (1) paired binary log loss, probability revision minus probability reference, and (2) paired movement MSE, movement revision minus movement reference. Evaluate all eligible immutable forecasts with available targets, not just bets. Report the raw `q` probability benchmark and zero-movement benchmark descriptively, plus Brier score, fixed reliability bins `[0,.1),...,[.9,1]` and missing-target counts. No benchmark or diagnostic selects a model after observing results.

For both primary model comparisons use **10,000 paired whole-week bootstrap draws, NumPy PCG64 seed 20260909, and 97.5% two-sided percentile intervals**. Sort active paired-forecast weeks by the kickoff's Eastern Monday date; for each draw sample `G` week indices with replacement from those `G` weeks. Resample the two models together and compute the difference as the sum of sampled within-week per-game loss differences divided by the sum of sampled game counts. Reinitialize this fixed generator separately for each target; use quantiles `.0125` and `.9875` with linear interpolation. The same draw count/seed/resampling definition applies to ROI using active locked-position weeks and unit-risk denominators, with quantiles `.025/.975` and `.005/.995`. With fewer than two contributing weeks or no denominator, withhold an inferential interval and report the observations. Report elapsed calendar weeks and zero-observation weeks separately. Compute stable log loss from locked logits rather than clipping outcome-conditioned probabilities after the fact.

The 97.5% intervals provide a local Bonferroni sensitivity for these **two** model-score comparisons under the weekly-block approximation. They do not jointly cover profitability, all diagnostics, the four earlier policies or the project's prior research searches. Expanding training also induces dependence between prediction weeks, while teams and weather recur; the bootstrap approximation may understate uncertainty. A small positive paper return or favorable lower bound is insufficient by itself to claim a high-confidence edge. Any stronger claim at the fixed endpoint must disclose all comparisons, broader multiplicity, concentration and observed-offer execution limitations. An unsuccessful result remains part of the public record.

Changes to features, objectives, penalties, source cohorts, receipt windows, targets, selection or evaluation dates require a new exploratory version and separately recorded future evaluation. Routine repairs that preserve the frozen computations must be documented, tested and tied to code hashes. They do not authorize replacing an old decision or using a label before it was actually received.
