# Seven-day weather revision capture protocol

Protocol ID: `weather-revision-capture-pilot-v1`. Written before the first bulk capture. The [feasibility assessment](WEATHER_REVISION_CAPTURE_FEASIBILITY.md) and [schema-only endpoint preflight](weather_revision_endpoint_preflight.json) preceded this protocol. Those preflight requests remain separately labeled and do not count as pilot observations.

This is an operational data-collection pilot. It records whether original forecast responses and subsequently received sportsbook prices can be captured with sufficient coverage and provenance for a future, separately specified experiment. It performs no weather-rule classification, model fitting, outcome join, settlement, threshold search, EV calculation, ranking, pick publication or candidate promotion. The four registered policies, model forecasts, positions and daily strategy behavior remain unchanged.

## Fixed window and execution times

Include capture starts in **[2026-09-09 03:00:00 UTC, 2026-09-16 03:00:00 UTC)**. Both scheduled and actual manually initiated captures must satisfy this window. Scheduled attempts occur at **01:17, 07:17, 13:17 and 19:17 UTC**, giving 28 scheduled slots inside the window. The first in-window slot is September 9 at 07:17; the last is September 16 at 01:17. Manual captures have their own receipt and invocation identity and are reported separately.

Record scheduled slot when known, actual capture start/end, workflow run/attempt ID, trigger, code version and any execution delay. GitHub scheduling is best effort. A delayed run uses its actual start for all eligibility and initialization calculations; it must not be backdated to its scheduled slot. Missed attempts remain missing. No catch-up request may claim a forecast or quote receipt at the missed time. Jobs starting outside the window record or publish a skipped status and make no pilot forecast/quote request. A run admitted before the end may finish its already frozen bounded cohort afterward; every response retains its actual receipt time.

## Cohort fixed before weather values

At capture start, obtain and archive the official ESPN college-football FBS/group-80 schedule inventory for the next seven days. Use current official event/competition identity and confirmed pregame state. Define future as `capture_start < kickoff <= capture_start + 7 days`. Match exact game and home/away team IDs; ambiguous or duplicate identities are explicit failures, never a choice between convenient rows.

Sort qualifying official events by kickoff UTC, then numeric game ID, and take at most **150**. Apply this cap before weather, venue or sportsbook coverage is known. Record the full enumerated count, capped cohort, cap exclusions and inventory failures before retrieving weather. A schedule failure yields an incomplete run, not a falsely complete empty slate.

For every capped row, preserve actual event venue ID, kickoff, home/away team IDs, pregame state, neutral-site evidence and current roof evidence with their source receipts. Request weather only for confirmed outdoor, non-neutral contexts whose actual venue ID appears in the existing 100-venue catalog. Unknown roof/neutral state, unsupported venue, missing current venue metadata and unverified provider matching remain explicit missingness. They do not cause replacement by a later game beyond the cap. Do not infer the venue from the home team or use fuzzy matchup proximity to resolve ambiguity.

Catalog: `data/models/weather_venues_v1.json`; SHA-256 `eec79812c7faef8d70b21d9ad4018b3d2a71074b27b72508358d71ae56519ce7`. Coordinates are frozen. Current roof evidence establishes the current response's context, not a separately certified historical roof state.

Each stable game ID has a context version derived from its actual kickoff, venue ID, home/away identity and roof/neutral evidence. A reschedule or venue/identity change creates a new context version. Preserve the previous version and its observations; do not silently rewrite, combine, or retroactively relabel it. Provider-ID mappings retain their supporting official/provider event receipts and the matching rule/version.

## Exact forecast products

For every eligible venue, compute **`initialization = floor_to_6h(capture_start_utc − 6 hours)`**. Fetch that exact `gfs_global` run from the Single Runs endpoint. It is the cycle eligible under the fixed six-hour collection buffer, not a claim that it was the newest available cycle. Do not substitute a different initialization or product after failure.

Use the full-run request verified in preflight: fixed catalog latitude/longitude; `models=gfs_global`; explicit UTC `run`; `hourly=temperature_2m,relative_humidity_2m,wind_speed_10m`; `forecast_days=8`; Fahrenheit, mph and GMT. Do not send `start_hour` or `end_hour`, which the endpoint rejected. Record every supplied spatial/elevation option, including whether provider defaults were used, plus returned grid coordinates/elevation. A single-venue successful preflight does not establish universal coverage. [Single Runs API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api), [GFS API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/gfs-api).

Deduplicate identical `(venue, initialization, request options)` within a capture. Verify units, GMT offset, ordered unique valid times, aligned arrays and finite physically valid measurements. Require exact coverage of the kickoff-floor UTC hour and the next three hours. Extract kickoff-hour temperature/RH and all four scalar wind values, and record their arithmetic mean as a descriptive measurement only. No wind, temperature or humidity strategy threshold is applied.

Store actual lead hours and identify the hours beyond 120-hour lead, where the provider's hourly series can interpolate native three-hour GFS output. Do not present the long-lead hourly values as independently issued native hourly forecasts. The preflight returned 192 hours through initialization +191h, sufficient nominal horizon for the seven-days-from-start cohort and four-hour game window; every individual response still requires a completeness check.

Archive `previous_day2` separately as a fixed-lead comparator only when mature. Define maturity as `floor_UTC_hour(kickoff) + 3h − 48h + 6h`. Determine request eligibility from capture start, and require the actual successful receipt to be at or after maturity. An earlier provisional receipt can never become mature merely because time passed. A reused mature observation retains its original receipt and explicit cache reference. Do not stamp an inferred single initialization onto this comparator: its four valid hours may originate from different cycles. [Previous Runs API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api).

Fetch the explicit run anew across different captures, even when the same run was requested previously, so same-run response changes remain observable. Only identical venue/run requests within one capture are deduplicated. Every distinct response body is retained. Repeated unchanged bodies can share a content-addressed blob but require distinct request receipts. A changed body for the same venue/product/run creates another immutable receipt; preserve the earliest successful receipt and all observed changes. The mature previous-day comparator alone may reuse its earliest successful context-matched receipt; this must not manufacture a fresh network receipt. The provider body did not echo an independently verifiable initialization field in preflight; requested run, endpoint semantics and valid-time range are recorded evidence, not independent proof of original model vintage or publication time.

## Quotes follow weather

After weather acquisition and explicit weather failures are recorded, recheck the current official event summary for weather-available contexts before fetching quotes, capped at 150 extra summary requests. Compare the current game/team, kickoff, venue, pregame and neutral context with the frozen version. Preserve changes or recheck failures and withhold a context-consistent association when the check fails; do not rewrite the earlier weather context. This recheck budget is fixed before bulk collection.

Then fetch new authoritative DraftKings and FanDuel observations for the frozen cohort. Preserve each provider event ID and both sides of every valid full-game total pair with the same sportsbook, total line and market period. Store exact offered line, decimal and original American prices where supplied, provider market-update time, request interval and actual response receipt. A partial pair, nonfinite price, mismatched side line, missing book or ambiguous event mapping remains unavailable.

Do not shop additional sportsbooks, choose a favorable pair, apply the daily signal's price/EV gates, or substitute an earlier saved quote. Multiple reported main lines or duplicate/conflicting pairs require explicit handling and retained provenance rather than an outcome-driven selection. Quote records remain useful even when weather is unavailable; that missingness must stay visible.

Link each valid weather/quote association to its exact context and immutable receipts. Record the weather-to-quote receipt gap. A same-run quote used as contemporaneous accompaniment must have been received after the linked weather response; retained older quote snapshots are not retroactively paired with later weather as information available at the earlier quote. Original market-update timestamps are distinct from fresh full-state observations and are never rewritten as receipt times. If a game begins or its context changes during collection, retain the responses but flag that association as no longer a verified pregame/context-consistent pair.

## Immutable provenance, failures and limits

Archive original unparsed response bodies before deriving tables, using SHA-256 and content-addressed gzip storage. The receipt schema includes `requested_at`, `received_at`, `status_code`, `body_sha256`, `body_path`, `receipt_path`, sanitized `request{url,params}` and `response_headers`. Record available HTTP Date/ETag/Last-Modified/content-type/content-encoding headers, stored-body size and any transport/parser failure. If the HTTP client decodes transfer compression, distinguish stored unparsed body length from wire Content-Length rather than treating them as necessarily identical. Never publish API keys, authorization/cookie headers or credential-bearing URLs. Parsed rows carry parser/code/catalog/protocol hashes, context version and parent body/receipt IDs. Completed `runs/*.json` manifests are immutable. A mutable operational status pointer may refer to them without altering their contents.

Run states are `ok`, `partial`, `failed`, `no_games` or `outside_pilot`; their explanations and explicit failures must be retained. `no_games` requires a successful official inventory yielding no in-window pregame games. Include `cohort_games`, `weather_available_games`, `two_book_games`, `paired_games`, `failed_requests` and `total_requests`, alongside exclusions and component status. `weather_available_games` means a validated explicit Single Runs response; mature previous-day comparator coverage is counted separately. `two_book_games` counts both DraftKings and FanDuel with valid same-book Over/Under pairs independently of weather availability. `paired_games` counts available weather with at least one valid same-book pair actually received after that weather; it does not imply both books. An optional `paired_two_book_games` counts the stricter intersection. Preserve single-book coverage rather than relabeling it as two-book coverage. An `ok` execution label does not by itself imply complete weather or sportsbook coverage; report the actual denominators.

Raw official/provider bodies can incidentally contain other fields; the pilot's derived tables must not extract final scores, outcomes or performance statistics. Collection files and ledgers stay separate from model forecasts, paper positions and closing-line settlement. Request and commit timestamps describe observed execution and provenance; they are not independently certified original provider publication times.

Use bounded requests with timeouts; this pilot's archive client makes no automatic retry or redirect. A separately recorded retry, if needed, remains the same request with its new actual timing. Do not change venue, cycle, field, book or product to repair a failure. Preserve incomplete runs and every missingness reason. Record actual usage/bytes. No subscription purchase or entitlement change is authorized by this protocol. A quota or transport failure is reported as incomplete, not converted into a successful empty capture.

The [quota preflight](weather_revision_quota_preflight.json) returned an actual `/events` limit of **100 requests/hour**, overriding the provider documentation's generic allowance for this account. Limit the pilot to **17 Odds API IO requests per capture** and reserve **20 remaining requests** for existing workflows. After the fresh event inventory, require reported remaining allowance at least `ceil(chosen_provider_events/10) + 20` before starting ten-event quote batches. Stop when reported remaining allowance is at or below 20, or on HTTP 401, 403 or 429. Missing or unparseable allowance is explicitly unavailable, not permission to assume a larger documented limit. Do not start quote batching without verified required headroom. Provider-side usage by other clients can still change between requests; retain all rate-limit headers and resulting failures.

Freeze the protocol hash and collection/parser/workflow versions before the first bulk capture. Implementation corrections during the pilot must preserve old receipts and record a new code/parser version plus a reason; they must not rewrite measurements from an earlier version. Any scope, cohort, product, schedule or analysis change requires a separately recorded protocol version before affected collection or analysis.

## Pilot completion report

At the end, report operational coverage only: 28 expected scheduled slots, actual scheduled/manual attempts, delays, complete/incomplete/missed runs, official/capped/venue-supported cohorts, context changes, exact-run response coverage, mature-comparator coverage, valid two-book/single-book/missing quote coverage, paired receipt-gap distribution, failures by reason, duplicate or changed same-run bodies, request usage and storage bytes. Recompute denominators from immutable inventories and manifests. A quiet or failed run cannot be counted as perfect coverage.

Check a fixed, reproducible sample of context/forecast/quote associations against original bodies and validate all hashes and timing invariants. Publish operational limitations and parser/version differences. Do not join game outcomes, calculate forecast accuracy or returns, test revision signals, claim positive EV, promote a candidate or alter the four-policy prospective evaluation. The resulting archive can support a future research plan written separately before its own analysis.
