# ACC availability and subsequent prices: fixed collection pilot

Protocol `acc-availability-capture-v1`, September 9, 2026. This is a collection protocol, not a betting policy. The [source preflight](ACC_CURRENT_AVAILABILITY_PREFLIGHT.md), [verified conference policy](ACC_AVAILABILITY_POLICY_2026.md) and [unfrozen directional research proposal](AVAILABILITY_PROSPECTIVE_DESIGN_DRAFT.md) precede collection. No status coefficients, outcomes, movement labels, EV calculations or paper positions are produced by this pilot. Existing candidates remain unchanged.

## Window and timing

Admit actual capture starts in **[September 9, 2026 08:00 UTC, September 16, 2026 07:00 UTC)**. Scheduled slots are `:17` and `:47` at hours 00, 01, 02 and 10 through 23, plus 06:17, in **America/New_York**. This fixed grid has **245 slots** inside the window, from September 9 at 06:17 Eastern through September 16 at 02:47 Eastern. It replaces the draft's conditional game-day grid with a simpler fixed schedule. The two daily grids overlap neither each other nor the existing `:07/:37` quote schedule. Execution can still overlap or be delayed by other workflows.

The overnight grid covers 20:00 game-location deadlines in Eastern through Pacific time, and the daytime grid samples game-day updates. It does not certify the exact time a report was published or guarantee an immediate response to news. GitHub dispatch is best effort. Record actual start/end, run ID, attempt, trigger and code hash; do not backdate a delayed/manual run or replace a missed slot with a later observation. Manual captures are permitted and separately labeled. Starts outside the window make no source or price requests. The window must be frozen in Git before the first pilot request; earlier schema preflights are not pilot observations.

## Sources, cohort and identity

First archive an official ESPN FBS/group-80 inventory for the next seven days. Reuse the existing pregame identity parser and its 150-game inventory cap, ordered by kickoff then game ID. This inventory is frozen before the availability request. Then make the exact public ACC eligibility POST verified in preflight. Only an explicit `public:true` response permits the fixed `get-publish-public` POST. No private/archive endpoint, cookie, subscription or authentication is needed for these two calls. The access response is capped at 1 MiB; the current response at 64 MiB because the observed payload repeats approximately 1 MiB of presentation imagery per report. Partial, oversized, non-JSON, HTTP and transport failures remain failures with their actual receipts.

Retain every current report's original bytes. Extract only the observed metadata contract: opaque report key, literal ReportType, source date/time/timezone, two team-name blocks and raw row-array lengths. Do not interpret an unknown populated row schema or treat its length as verified injury counts. Classify `Report Pending` as pending and every other phase as **nonpending, completion unverified** until a separately recorded schema audit establishes the completed marker. Empty pending/unknown arrays do not mean everyone is available. No semantic player revision is inferred from raw-body hashes; imagery or formatting can change independently of player status.

Match the unordered source team pair to exactly one frozen official game using exact normalized official team `location`, `displayName`, `shortDisplayName` or `abbreviation` values. Preserve which official values support the match. Do not use prefix/fuzzy matching or infer home/away from the source block order. Require distinct team identities, a unique upcoming match and literal source date/time agreement with the official kickoff interpreted on the source's `ET` basis as America/New_York. This is an explicit operational interpretation, not independent certification of the feed's clock semantics; disagreements remain unresolved rather than silently shifting dates. Provider issue labels stay literal and separate from actual HTTP receipt.

All matched reports are included regardless of pending state, row contents or prices. Deduplicate their official game IDs, order by kickoff/game ID and cap at **20 games before fetching odds**. Preserve unmatched reports, duplicates, cap exclusions and zero-report responses. An empty or missing ACC response is not proof that there are no ACC games. Optional nonconference reports are retained; conference classification and primary modeling eligibility are deferred. Roof and neutral-site exclusions from the weather experiment do not apply.

## Prices and original receipts

After the availability receipt and identity reconciliation, recheck the official event summary for each matched game, at most 20 requests with four workers. Require the same official event/team IDs, kickoff and pregame state; no roof or venue gate is added. Then obtain fresh observations from the existing selected DraftKings/FanDuel account for successful rechecks. Reuse the seven-day provider matching and exact same-book full-game Over/Under parser. Request at most four IO calls: selected books, provider inventory and two ten-game batches. The entire capture makes at most 27 HTTP requests. Respect the existing 20-request reserve and actual rate-limit metadata, including failures or missing required inventory allowance. Do not add books, buy access, substitute old quotes or choose games by modeled value. One valid paired book is useful; the two-book intersection is reported separately.

Keep every valid total pair, exact decimal prices, provider event ID, original market update time, request start and response receipt. Link a pair only when receipt order is report request/receipt, official recheck request/receipt, quote request/receipt, all before kickoff. Price requests re-establish the exact provider home/away, kickoff and pregame state against the frozen official identity. A changed kickoff or ambiguous provider mapping leaves the association unavailable; earlier context is never rewritten. Report-to-quote gaps are measurements, not guarantees of execution or sportsbook stake acceptance.

Use separate `data/runtime/availability` bodies, receipts, cohorts and immutable run manifests. Preserve exact transfer-decoded response bytes in content-addressed gzip files and distinguish them from wire Content-Length. Record original public request method/body, UTC request/receipt times, SHA-256, response status and explicit incomplete/error states. ACC POST receipts include safe Date/Age/Cache-Control/ETag/Last-Modified headers; shared GET receipts retain their existing Date/ETag/Last-Modified and rate-limit fields. Fresh receipt does not defeat provider caching. Never publish account keys or cookie/auth headers. Shared GET archival code retains its existing credential-echo protection; ACC POST requests use no account secrets.

Every completed run records source/parser/protocol/workflow hashes, selected cohort, exact receipt references, pending/unknown/matched report counts, quote coverage and all failures. A successful transport run can contain zero verified completed reports. Public status must distinguish those facts. Preserve earlier attempts and raw payloads when parsers change; later reprocessing cannot manufacture a historical model forecast or paper entry.

## End-of-pilot decision

Publish operational counts and missed/delayed/manual observations against the 245-slot denominator; inspect actual original bodies and timing/identity joins. Before any movement or directional fit, audit a populated report and freeze its completion/phase/player mapping and the relevant role source. The linked draft is not a registered model: its proposed QB transitions, sample minimum, target and fit still require that work. Do not inspect future movement/outcome labels to select the mapping or thresholds.

The pilot can establish whether timely reports and later prices are observable. It cannot establish profitable betting. A season extension, movement analysis or probability/paper policy needs its own fixed protocol before its data are analyzed for that purpose.
