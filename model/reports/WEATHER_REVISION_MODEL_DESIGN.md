# Two falsifiable weather-revision candidates

Design date: September 9, 2026 UTC. **Recommendation: prioritize a small, market-anchored probability model for the offered total; use a six-hour line-movement model as a secondary diagnostic. Neither is fitted or approved for live publication.** This document specifies proposed estimation rather than changing the collection-only pilot or the [four-policy prospective evaluation](PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md). No outcomes, forecast revisions or model-performance targets were joined or inspected for this design.

## What the existing evidence rules out

The [NOAA replication](NOAA_WEATHER_RESULTS_AUDIT.md) produced 68 wins and 62 losses in 130 fixed-rule selections, approximately −0.14% at assumed −110, with a wide interval crossing zero. It does not supply a reliable Under probability or a points-per-mph coefficient. Its historical total references also lack executable morning price receipts.

The [58-feature experiment](ORDINARY_MODEL_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md) found both new ridge and boosting configurations worse than the market and existing ridge in the selection and reused-2025 periods. The [distribution/calibration experiment](CALIBRATION_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md) likewise found no replacement probability model. Adding a large weather/statistics feature library, transferring old fitted coefficients, or repeatedly searching weather cutoffs would reintroduce those problems.

The pilot contributes a different input: original, dated forecasts followed by dated prices. The [hosted audit](WEATHER_REVISION_HOSTED_CAPTURE_AUDIT.md) verifies 46 weather-covered games out of 86, with 16 weather timeouts; 44 games have at least one later book pair. These are overlapping observations of games, not independent football weeks. The first two audited captures requested the same GFS initialization; they are response-consistency observations, not a six-hour initialization revision. A seven-day pilot cannot by itself establish a market-beating weather relationship.

## Shared observation contract

Use only exact, unchanged game/venue/team/kickoff/roof/neutral contexts and the frozen venue coordinates. Both weather observations must be independently received explicit `gfs_global` runs with the same request options and game-valid hours. Use kickoff-hour temperature and RH, and the arithmetic mean of four hourly scalar winds. Define a revision only between initializations exactly six hours apart. A changed response body for the same initialization is a separate provenance diagnostic and never substitutes for this revision.

For the first study, prescribe one decision per game: the first **scheduled** capture whose nominal slot is 24–48 hours before the currently confirmed kickoff, using the immediately preceding scheduled slot as the older observation. Require actual receipt ordering, an actual capture separation of 4–8 hours, and an actual decision still 24–48 hours before kickoff. Exclude missing scheduled pairs; do not search later captures for a favorable revision or result. Manual probes remain outside this primary sample. Freeze the designated slots from the official schedule before retrieving their weather; a reschedule invalidates that context rather than selecting a replacement slot after observing its results.

Let `t0` and `t1` denote the old and decision observations. Require a unique valid main DraftKings Over/Under pair at each, and a unique valid FanDuel main pair at `t1`. Both books must be freshly observed after the `t1` weather and successful context recheck. Multiple main lines, unavailable sides, or uncertain full-game settlement are excluded with reasons. These criteria choose an analysis contract, not the most profitable historical line. Do not forward-fill an earlier quote through a suspension or infer continued availability from an isolated old tick.

The information cutoff is the actual decision quote receipt. Everything used as a predictor must have a receipt at or before that cutoff. Preserve kickoff lead, initialization age, weather-to-quote gap, source/version, and all exclusions. When both book responses have different receipts, the decision clock is their maximum; an eventual paper signal needs a final fresh check of its chosen offer.

For period `j`, define the reference line `L_j` as the unique DraftKings total and the price-balance reference

`q_j = (1 / d_under,j) / [(1 / d_under,j) + (1 / d_over,j)]`.

This is a proportional removal of that book's margin, **not an assertion that its probability is true**. FanDuel prices at a different line are not votes on the same event. Its contemporaneous line difference can be a fitted predictor; do not average different-line probabilities or manufacture an odds-to-points conversion.

The shared baseline predictors `C` are: current `L_1`; actual hours to kickoff; current wind, temperature and RH; `L_1 − L_0`; `logit(q_1) − logit(q_0)`; and the current FanDuel-minus-DraftKings line difference. The price-balance difference is a descriptive market-history feature, not a fixed-contract price return when the line moved. The revision block is exactly

`R = (wind_1 − wind_0, temperature_1 − temperature_0, RH_1 − RH_0)`.

No weather thresholds, sign constraints, team interactions, lag search, new weather fields or score/ROI-selected feature selection are proposed. Including current weather levels and recent price behavior in the comparator prevents a revision model from receiving credit merely for rediscovering weather levels or market momentum.

## Candidate 1: probability of beating the exact offered total

**Question:** does `R` improve the conditional probability of the final score finishing below the current offered line, after accounting for `q_1` and `C`?

Start with half-point reference totals only. For final official total `Y`, use `Z = 1[Y < L_1]`. The predicted distribution at that exact contract is `(P(Under), P(push), P(Over)) = (p, 0, 1−p)`. This estimates one relevant point of the final-score CDF; it does not claim a coherent full score distribution or extrapolate to another line. Integer lines remain unavailable until a separately validated push model exists.

Fit the reference and challenger on identical eligible games:

`reference: logit(p_B) = logit(q_1) + alpha_B + beta_B' C`

`revision:  logit(p_R) = logit(q_1) + alpha_R + beta_R' C + gamma' R`.

Estimate parameters by minimizing mean binary log loss plus `0.1 / 2` times the sum of squared fitted coefficients, including the intercept. The offset coefficient is fixed at one. Standardize each predictor using training-only means and population standard deviations, use scale one for constant columns, and clip standardized predictors to `[−5, 5]` using that same training transform. No imputation of missing forecast/quote pairs and no penalty grid. These are explicit regularization choices, not claimed physical effect sizes. Retain the uncalibrated `q_1` as an additional descriptive benchmark. A failed optimizer yields unavailable predictions, not favorable fallback bets.

The primary comparison is paired out-of-sample binary log loss, revision minus the fitted reference. Brier score, reliability by fixed probability bins and calibration intercept/slope are diagnostics. Report score differences on every eligible forecast, not just predicted bets. A coefficient's sign or in-sample likelihood improvement does not establish an edge.

**Route to an actual paper signal:** after fitting, freezing and separately registering a future paper policy, evaluate only genuinely observed offers at `L_1`. At decimal price `d`, expected unit returns are `d*p_R − 1` for Under and `d*(1−p_R) − 1` for Over. If FanDuel offers this exact line, its price may be considered; a different line cannot use the same `p_R`. Select at most one side/book per game by the largest positive estimated EV, with a predetermined tie order, otherwise abstain. Lock the final quote and probability, risk one flat paper unit, and preserve later adverse movement. No Kelly staking, guessed edge multiplier, confidence label, or threshold optimization is justified. This formula produces a testable model-based signal; it does not establish that the estimated probability is accurate.

Falsify the probability hypothesis if revisions fail to improve held-out proper scores against the shared comparator, if apparent improvement is concentrated in one week/venue or disappears at actual receipt times, or if selected paper returns fail the separately registered profitability test. Positive proper-score improvement without positive returns is not success on the user's profitability objective.

## Candidate 2: residual six-hour market movement

**Question:** after the current market has already been observed, do forecast revisions predict an additional same-book total-line adjustment by the next scheduled capture?

Use the same designated `t0,t1` game sample, including integer lines for this target if their market identity is otherwise valid. Define the target as `M = L_2 − L_1`, where `L_2` is DraftKings' unique main full-game line at the immediately next scheduled capture, with actual receipt 4–8 hours after `t1` and before kickoff. Require the unchanged context. A missing next quote is missing; no later quote or closing value replaces it. Report whether target availability depends on starting weather and price features.

Fit one ridge regression on `C` and one on `(C,R)`. Use mean squared error plus `0.1 / 2` times squared coefficients, the same training-only predictor transformation as Candidate 1, and a training-standardized target. An unpenalized intercept is permitted here; a zero training target variance returns the constant training mean. Fixed parameters and one horizon replace a lag/threshold search. Compare paired future MSE against the shared no-revision reference and against zero movement. A line-only target deliberately misses repricing that changes only juice; report that limitation instead of silently converting prices to line points.

This model can be estimated before games finish, but many transitions in one football week do not create many independent market environments. A successful directional or MSE result is evidence of delayed price adjustment at the observed horizon. **It is not an EV model and cannot publish a betting pick on its own.** A forecast that an Under line will fall does not specify its win probability, vig, fillability, or whether the current offer is still overpriced. Use it to challenge the slow-adjustment mechanism and, only under a later preregistered policy, to study decision timing. Do not multiply its predicted line movement by a borrowed score-residual coefficient to declare profit.

## Estimation, separation and falsification dates

Before any labels are read, inventory dated coverage and freeze exact training/evaluation calendar boundaries, source cohorts, and designated slots. Keep every observation of a game in one fold and all games from an Eastern Monday–Sunday week together. Use expanding earlier-week training; target completion must precede the training cutoff. Never randomly split repeated game captures. Standardization and all fitted parameters use training data only.

A practical minimum for fitting the proposed small models is 300 distinct games spanning at least 12 completed weeks. This is an estimation safeguard, not a confidence guarantee or permission to stop when significant. Without validated older history, a concrete prospective path is approved collection through January 31, 2027 for development, then a frozen next-season test whose endpoint is registered before its first labels. If that training set misses the minimum, record insufficient data. The seven-day pilot does not automatically extend into this collection. A shorter archive can support software and coverage diagnostics, but no confident edge estimate.

If older genuinely dated coverage is validated, earlier whole seasons can provide training and a later whole season a development check; all previously examined seasons remain reused development. Do not convert such reuse into a fresh holdout by assigning a new label. The two primary incremental comparisons are Candidate 1 log loss and Candidate 2 movement MSE. Prespecify 10,000 paired whole-week bootstrap draws and 97.5% two-sided intervals for these two comparisons as a local multiplicity sensitivity. Report all results, calendar/active-week counts, venue and team concentration, leave-one-week-out estimates, missingness and book/source diagnostics. That adjustment does not cover the project's unknown wider search or prove independent weekly blocks.

Profitability needs its own fixed future evaluation of locked offers and settlement, including vig, pushes/voids where applicable, adverse missing-outcome sensitivity and observed-versus-accepted-price limitations. Do not combine new revision-policy returns with the four existing candidates. For scale, a unit-return standard deviation around 0.95 requires roughly 1,400 independent bets just to reduce a normal-approximation 95% interval's half-width to five percentage points. Detecting a true 5% ROI with about 80% power would require roughly 2,800 under those simplified assumptions; dependence and multiple testing increase the requirement. Hundreds of forecasts can fit a small model without proving a modest profitable edge.

## Historical availability and the latency problem

The movements endpoint is documented per event, book, market and handicap line. Its published example uses generic side fields and timestamps; it does not by itself resolve historic retention, all-line enumeration, suspension continuity or pregame/executable timing. Complete the [source validation](../../docs/ODDS_MOVEMENT_FEASIBILITY.md) before using such records. Do not enumerate only the eventual closing line: that would select the retrospective contract using future market behavior. History retrieved today must remain labeled provider-reported history rather than a receipt actually collected then. [Official movement reference](https://docs.odds-api.io/api-reference/odds/get-odds-movements).

If historical timestamp or retention validation fails, train these candidates only from future original receipts after the separately authorized collection period. Neither closing-only odds nor the existing NOAA closing-total residuals can identify what was mispriced at an earlier forecast revision. The Open-Meteo GFS Single Runs archive starts in April 2026, limiting how much old college-football training it can supply; older NOAA runs would need a separate source/timing study and still require dated entry prices. No ECMWF hindcast substitution is proposed. [Single Runs documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api).

At the scheduled pilot slots, the selected initialization is **7 hours 17 minutes old before scheduler/network delay**. The provider describes typical global-run availability around 4–6 hours after initialization. Consequently, this pilot may miss a fast reaction by roughly one to three hours; Candidates 1 and 2 would initially test remaining slow adjustment, not access to newly released information. Actual hosted weather-to-price gaps also reached 130 seconds. These delays must remain part of the training/deployment regime. [Single Runs timing](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api).

A later, distinct latency protocol could record the relevant model's metadata transition and the first successfully received, validated explicit-run fields, then fetch fresh prices. Archive previous and new metadata, field responses and actual request intervals. Modification time is not API availability; availability can vary across redundant servers. The provider recommends an additional ten minutes for consistency. Metadata and a successful request document what this client observed, not global first dissemination or what sportsbooks knew. Never backdate a decision to initialization plus six hours. Faster collection would change the observation regime and require a separately registered validation, not a retroactive improvement to this pilot. [Model-update metadata documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/model-updates).

## Can unexpected realized weather identify the mechanism?

Potentially it can help study a physical scoring response, but it is **not a third deployable candidate here**. Define a hypothetical shock as actual stadium-representative weather minus its conditional expectation from information available at the decision. Estimating that expectation from prior-only meteorological records, then relating held-out shocks to final-score surprises, could be more informative about unanticipated weather than a regression on forecast levels alone.

However, `actual − one GFS forecast` is not automatically an exogenous shock. Station-versus-stadium error, persistent model bias, omitted newer forecasts, terrain, seasonality, anticipated coaching changes and source-dependent missingness can correlate that difference with scoring. Weather windows must be fixed by scheduled valid hours, not actual game duration, which scoring and weather can themselves affect. The required data include stable outdoor-venue evidence, matched quality-controlled observed wind/temperature/RH with consistent units/heights, all issued forecasts used to define the information set, and many prior weather hours for chronological bias correction. Reanalysis is not a direct stadium observation, and a forecast error is not an instrument merely because it is called a surprise.

Identification would require an explicit conditional mean-independence assumption for the remaining nonweather scoring shock, measurement-error treatment, and transportability of the physical response across venues and forecast regimes. Those assumptions are not established by existing inputs. Even a valid physical coefficient only describes scoring's response to weather; it does not reveal how much the current sportsbook price already incorporates. Realized weather is unavailable at betting time. Converting a revision into EV would still require estimating the change in the conditional weather distribution and validating the resulting final-outcome probabilities **against the contemporaneous entry market**. Reject this route as an immediate coefficient shortcut; pursue it only as a separately costed mechanism study if the primary receipt-based models warrant it.

The next concrete modeling step is therefore a label-free manifest of eligible consecutive-run observations and exact contracts, followed by a frozen estimation protocol once dated coverage is known. No coefficients, probabilities, selections or active policies are produced by this design document.
